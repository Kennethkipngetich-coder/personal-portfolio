Project 2: Online Learning Platform

Status: Proposed

A web platform that enables students to access course materials anytime from anywhere. Students can register, view their course units, read lesson notes, download study resources and take short quizzes to test their understanding. Lecturers can also upload materials and also track students progress. The platform makes learning more flexible and accessible.

  • Student registration and login
  • Course units with lesson notes
  • Downloadable study materials
  • Quizzes and assessments
  • Progress tracking

Tools Used: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and MySQL
